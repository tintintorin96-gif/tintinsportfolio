# Motion Design — Research Lens

Use for **motion language** on shipped web experiences.

## What to study

- Easing, staging, and choreography
- Scroll-linked reveals with reduced-motion fallbacks
- Hover and transition systems that stay portfolio-safe

_Updated by the Experience Signal Scout each week._

## Latest weekly research — 2026-W24

- Motion is acting as direction and governance: scroll advances chapters, hover clarifies affordances, and progress cues keep the visitor oriented.
- The preferred feel is cinematic but controlled, with one or two slow reveal beats instead of constant ambient movement.
- Reduced-motion behavior should preserve the same sequence through instant state changes and static depth cues.
