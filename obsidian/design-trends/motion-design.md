- Scroll-linked reveals with reduced-motion fallbacks

## Latest weekly research - 2026-W28

**Signal window:** 2026-06-29 - 2026-07-05

- Motion language is shifting toward sequence control: ScrollTrigger, Lenis-synchronized scrolling, Lottie, and visual GSAP tooling are recurring across award sites and production tooling.
- Podium's strongest lesson is restraint: soft directional transitions, scroll as pacing, character-level reveals used sparingly, and effects removed when they distracted from media.
- AI-native products add a second motion role: state visibility. Streaming, tool-call, and approval states need calm progress cues instead of decorative loaders.
- Portfolio fit: subtle rail progression, crossfade transitions, and depth reveals; no flashing, scroll lock, or aggressive cursor trails.
