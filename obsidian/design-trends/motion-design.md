# Motion Design — Research Lens

Use for **motion language** on shipped web experiences.

## What to study

- Easing, staging, and choreography
- Scroll-linked reveals with reduced-motion fallbacks
- Hover and transition systems that stay portfolio-safe

## Latest weekly research — 2026-W28

- Motion signals from award/product examples favor cinematic state changes: reveals behave like calibration, confirmation, or scene progression.
- Agent UI patterns add a second motion language: streaming, tool progress, approval pauses, and state deltas must be legible and non-aggressive.
- Portfolio implication: choose subtle cinematic scroll and depth-reveal hover; reduced motion should show immediate hierarchy and state with no dependency on animated progress.
