# Motion Design — Research Lens

Use for **motion language** on shipped web experiences.

## What to study

- Easing, staging, and choreography
- Scroll-linked reveals with reduced-motion fallbacks
- Hover and transition systems that stay portfolio-safe

## Latest weekly research

_Updated by the Experience Signal Scout each week._

## Latest weekly research - 2026-W26

- Motion systems are moving toward authorable choreography: SplitText, staggered entry, timeline scrubbing, and design-variable animation.
- Award sites continue to use chapter intros and scroll reveals, but the strongest examples tie motion to state or story beats.
- Portfolio implication: subtle cinematic scroll is appropriate; snap sections plus expressive motion remains too forceful for a content-stable portfolio.
