## Latest weekly research - 2026-W26

- Experimental portfolio language is shifting from "enter my world" to "inspect my system": visible state, progress, and evidence can carry personality without hiding content.
- AI and robotics examples suggest technical ambition feels stronger when it is explainable and companionable.
- Portfolio implication: use precise state chips, rails, and scroll assembly in the hero; avoid chat overlays or cursor gimmicks.
