# Experimental Portfolios — Research Lens

Use for **ambitious portfolio and studio sites** that remain usable.

## What to study

- Hero as experimental canvas
- Spatial hierarchy and cursor/scroll models
- Balance of novelty vs. clarity

## Latest weekly research — 2026-W24

- Experimental portfolios are strongest when they are world-like but not maze-like: spatial cues, chaptered scroll, and clear project access.
- A recurring pattern is 2D interface clarity placed over a subtle 3D or depth layer.
- Avoid full-site game metaphors unless navigation, text contrast, and project discovery remain effortless.
