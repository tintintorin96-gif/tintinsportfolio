## Latest weekly research — 2026-W24

- Creative developer portfolios recognized across CSSDA-adjacent galleries are balancing Swiss-like typographic order with restrained WebGL or shader depth.
- Kinetic type and shader accents work best when the base 2D hierarchy remains strong and accessible.
- Avoid turning every section into an effect; supporting pages should inherit surface rhythm, not full hero spectacle.
