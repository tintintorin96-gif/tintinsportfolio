## Latest weekly research - 2026-W28

**Signal window:** 2026-06-29 - 2026-07-05

- Webflow's GSAP-powered Interactions coverage shows SplitText, ScrollTrigger, staggered timelines, reusable interactions, and visual easing controls becoming production-accessible.
- The important movement is not novelty animation; it is motion parity. Designers can now ship studio-grade text and scroll choreography without bespoke JavaScript for every effect.
- Portfolio fit: tokenized motion should feel current and precise, but the weekly system should stay subtle, accessible, and easy to reduce under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
