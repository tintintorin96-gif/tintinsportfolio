## Latest weekly research — 2026-W28

- Webflow/low-code portfolio references continue to lean on GSAP/Lenis scroll choreography, CMS-driven sections, and layered depth that feels more complex than the underlying structure.
- The strongest pattern is not maximal animation; it is authored scroll sequence with performance-conscious DOM structure and responsive fallbacks.
- Portfolio implication: apply cinematic scroll language to the hero and calm chapter rhythm to supporting sections; avoid smooth-scroll spectacle that interferes with reading.
