## Latest weekly research — 2026-W24

- Webflow's GSAP-backed interaction language points to motion becoming reusable and component-scoped rather than a set of one-off page tricks.
- Cinematic scroll components are popular, but the durable signal is the timeline system: stagger, progress, and reveal behavior can travel with components.
- Portfolio fit: tokenized cinematic scroll in the hero, simple reveal rhythm elsewhere, and a clear reduced-motion fallback.
