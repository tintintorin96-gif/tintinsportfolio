## Latest weekly research - 2026-W26

- Webflow's GSAP interaction direction points to motion becoming authorable: SplitText, scroll triggers, stagger controls, reusable timelines, and design-variable animation.
- The Horeca case study reinforces a practical split: build systematic motion visually where possible and reserve custom GSAP for genuinely complex depth, pivot, and sticky interactions.
- Portfolio implication: one controlled hero assembly sequence is more current than scattered bespoke animation across every section.
