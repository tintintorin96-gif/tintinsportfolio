## Analyst interpretation

**Dominant movement:** Living material stages — identity, archive, and process behave as one continuum of craft presence.

**Experience principles:**
- Typographic hierarchy can act as material, not only as message
- Archive fragments belong in the same stage as current work when subordinated to hierarchy
- Technical texture is a focus affordance, not a background effect
- Prestige reads through restraint and exact two-color discipline

**Emotional tone:** grounded, kinetic, craft-confident, industrially precise

**Avoid:** cream-editorial serif magazines, chat-dashboard mimicry, full-canvas illegibility, novelty overlays on supporting sections
