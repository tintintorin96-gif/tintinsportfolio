# Design trends research

Living research notes for the Weekly AI Creative Direction Engine.

## How this folder works

- **Lens files** (`awwwards.md`, `fwa.md`, etc.) — category lenses updated each week by the Experience Signal Scout with findings from the previous 7 days.
- **`YYYY-WXX.signals.md`** — consolidated weekly signal report (scout output).
- **`YYYY-WXX.pipeline.json`** — structured scout + analyst artifacts for audit.

Portfolio content lives in `obsidian/tintinportfolio/`. Weekly creative direction archives live in `creative-direction/archive/`.
