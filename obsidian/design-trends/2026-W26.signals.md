## Dominant movement

Inspectable narrative canvases. The week-level shift is not "AI visuals" or "more 3D"; it is a broader move toward interfaces that show how they are assembled. Framer Agents describe AI working directly on the canvas, A2UI-style protocols describe streaming native UI, Webflow/GSAP workflows make motion authorable, and award sites turn products into chaptered systems with visible states.

## Emerging signals

- **Canvas-native AI state as experience language** (AI-native products, shipped, emerging)
  - Why it matters: AI becomes trustworthy when its edits, state, and boundaries are visible.
  - Portfolio fit: Hero can behave like an inspectable command surface without adding a chat UI.

- **Agentic product personalities with controlled behavioral states** (Awwwards, shipped, emerging)
  - Why it matters: Elva's 30+ behavioral states suggest personality is strongest when encoded as system feedback.
  - Portfolio fit: Use state chips, progress rails, and tight hover feedback rather than character mascots.

- **Robotics and AI storytelling centered on coexistence** (Awwwards, shipped, emerging)
  - Why it matters: Fauna Robotics frames advanced technology through trust, companionship, and gentle clarity.
  - Portfolio fit: Keep the hero experimental but readable, with supporting sections that feel guided.

- **Sensory chapter systems** (Awwwards, shipped, recurring)
  - Why it matters: Indigo Laboratory uses chapters and sensory cues to turn a single page into a paced story.
  - Portfolio fit: Apply chapter-scroll pacing visually only; do not add autoplay sound.

- **Editorial fluidity with typographic grid discipline** (CSS Design Awards, shipped, recurring)
  - Why it matters: House of Honey shows fluid editorial presence can still score on UX and usability.
  - Portfolio fit: Supporting page treatment can use grid rhythm and precise surfaces while content stays unchanged.

- **Authorable motion systems replacing custom-code spectacle** (Webflow/Codrops, shipped, recurring)
  - Why it matters: Motion is becoming a reusable system, not a one-off effect stack.
  - Portfolio fit: One hero assembly sequence; subtle, reduced-motion-safe inheritance elsewhere.

## Opportunities

- Treat the hero as a live canvas with stable content, state rails, and evidence markers.
- Use a restrained luxury-tech palette: near-black foundation, warm luminous type, one mint signal accent.
- Let scroll reveal "system layers" rather than pushing users through snap sections.
- Make hover behavior clarify depth and affordance; do not make the cursor a spectacle.

## Risks / deprioritize

- AI mascots or blobs that feel decorative.
- Chat overlays that compete with navigation.
- Audio as required storytelling.
- Heavy WebGL or 3D depth across the full page.
- Randomized generative layouts that obscure portfolio hierarchy.
