# Interaction Lens

Last updated: 2026-06-08
Week: 2026-W23

## Current movement

Interaction quality is being judged by usefulness and pacing. The strongest experiences use scroll, hover, and reveal moments as a directed sequence rather than a bag of effects. Motion should make hierarchy and intent easier to understand.

## Evidence this week

- Lesse Studio's Codrops case study describes a move from atmospheric experimentation toward intuitive paths, repeated inquiry access, and subtle motion.
- Beaucoup's Codrops profile frames memorable experience as a coherent blend of storytelling, technology, motion, and visual precision.
- Awwwards and portfolio references continue to reward WebGL worlds and cinematic scroll narratives, but usability, performance, and responsive scores remain visible constraints.
- Webflow's portfolio guidance highlights minimal navigation, bold typography, and interactive storytelling as client-facing portfolio patterns.

## Portfolio implication

- Hero can behave like a calm single-take scene.
- Project and navigation surfaces should remain standard, scannable, and keyboard-friendly.
- Microinteractions should reinforce affordance: button focus, link hover, section reveal, and card depth.
- The weekly signature should be one memorable behavior, repeated consistently enough to feel intentional.

## Avoid

- Scroll hijacking.
- Cursor trails that distract from reading.
- Looped motion that continues after the user has understood the state.
- Interaction that depends on hover only.
