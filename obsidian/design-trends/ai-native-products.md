# AI-Native Products — Reference Lens

Use for **AI-native interface aesthetics** and emerging product interaction patterns.

## Sources

- Linear, Raycast, Arc Browser, Notion AI surfaces
- Emerging AI copilot and agent UIs
- Figma Community AI workflow patterns

## What to study

- How AI features are staged without overwhelming core tasks
- Typographic and spatial hierarchy for generative output
- Trust, latency, and state communication in AI flows
- Subtle motion for streaming / thinking / completion states

## Signal categories

- Interaction language for prompts, results, and undo
- Motion for progressive disclosure
- Visual systems that feel luxury-tech, not generic chat
- Creative technology opportunities (generative hero elements, adaptive layout)

## Weighting

Prefer shipped product patterns over concept mockups. Extract principles, not UI clones.
