# Creative Developer Portfolios — Reference Lens

Use for **experimental but usable** portfolio and showcase experiences.

## Sources

- Creative developer personal sites and studios
- Webflow Showcase, Framer Showcase
- GitHub / CodePen production-quality demos with clear UX intent

## What to study

- Hero-led composition as experimental canvas
- Scroll and cursor interaction without breaking readability
- Performance-conscious motion (GPU-friendly, reduced-motion fallbacks)
- Code-forward aesthetics that still feel art-directed

## Signal categories

- Interaction language (hover, scroll, spatial hierarchy)
- Motion language (easing, staging, restraint)
- Creative technology (Three.js, shaders, generative typography)
- Storytelling through sequence, not decoration

## Weighting

Favor portfolios that balance ambition with clarity — suitable for a content-stable living portfolio.
