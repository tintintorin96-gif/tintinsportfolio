## Emerging signals

- **Scroll as camera-state controller** (interaction-motion, shipped, recurring)
  - Why it matters: production portfolios increasingly use scroll as a predictable way to move through spatial chapters.
  - Portfolio fit: hero-as-prologue, with supporting sections inheriting only rhythm and surface treatment.

- **Reusable motion systems** (motion-system, shipped, emerging)
  - Why it matters: Webflow/GSAP component timelines indicate motion is becoming a design-system layer, not page-specific polish.
  - Portfolio fit: keep motion tokenized and subtle, with reduced-motion fallback as a first-class behavior.

- **Governed AI-native interface language** (ai-native-interface, mixed, emerging)
  - Why it matters: agentic products are making execution visible through traces, structured outputs, and control points.
  - Portfolio fit: translate this into visual inspection rails and status-like micro details without adding new product functionality.

- **Luxury-tech restraint** (visual-system, shipped, recurring)
  - Why it matters: premium sites are using fewer effects with stronger pacing, contrast, and material cues.
  - Portfolio fit: dark field, copper accent, crisp type, and one controlled atmospheric layer in the hero.

## Opportunities

- Frame the hero as a cinematic signal observatory: bold type, dark canvas, subtle coordinate depth, copper scan accents.
- Let hover interactions feel like inspection rather than decoration: depth reveal, trace line, or subtle surface lift.
- Keep navigation stable and flat; let the experimental energy live in the hero and section rhythm.
- Use a static coordinate/grain fallback for reduced motion and low-power contexts.

## Deprioritize this week

- Full-page game worlds.
- Unbounded generative UI metaphors.
- Heavy particle atmospheres.
- Serif magazine styling that conflicts with the owner baseline.
