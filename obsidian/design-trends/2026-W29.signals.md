## Direction opportunity

Build a cool, high-contrast hero as a **choreographed context field**: oversized type remains the stable anchor while a narrow sequence of indexed planes shifts focus, depth, and provenance through one restrained motion thread. Supporting sections inherit the index lines, cool neutral field, and cobalt focus cue without inheriting the spatial behavior.
