# Luxury Digital Experiences — Reference Lens

Use for **premium, high-contrast, intentional** digital brand experiences.

## Sources

- Luxury fashion, hospitality, and cultural institution sites
- Stripe, Vercel, and refined B2B product marketing with cinematic restraint
- Editorial-adjacent layouts that maintain AA contrast and usability

## What to study

- Typographic dominance and negative space as luxury signals
- Motion restraint — choreography over spectacle
- Single-accent color discipline
- Navigation stability with subtle treatment shifts

## Signal categories

- Visual language (contrast, scale, material)
- Motion language (slow ease, staged reveals)
- Storytelling systems (chaptered scroll, narrative hierarchy)
- Supporting page calm vs. hero expressiveness

## Portfolio fit

Extract mood and hierarchy principles for a hero-led system. Never import warm cream editorial palettes or serif display unless contrast-safe and on-brand.
