## Latest weekly research — 2026-W24

- WebGL, React Three Fiber, GSAP, and shader work remain visible in leading examples, but performance-aware projects increasingly fake depth with planes, compressed textures, and deliberate fallback states.
- AI-assisted assets and shader experimentation are useful as production inputs only when the final experience has human-led art direction.
- Portfolio fit: suggest a lightweight coordinate field or atmospheric layer through tokens rather than requiring a new rendering stack.
