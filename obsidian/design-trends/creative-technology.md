# Creative Technology — Research Lens

Use for **generative, WebGL, and code-forward experiences** that ship.

## What to study

- GPU-friendly effects with performance budgets
- Generative typography and adaptive layout ideas
- Creative tech that supports storytelling, not spectacle alone

## Latest weekly research - 2026-W26

- Creative technology signals favor systems that expose state: canvas-native AI edits, declarative UI streams, and interaction timelines that designers can inspect.
- WebGL and depth remain valuable when they support narrative, but recent production references warn against heavy effects outside the core story surface.
- Portfolio implication: use lightweight CSS/interaction tokens for the hero canvas and avoid site-wide 3D or particle systems.
