## Latest weekly research — 2026-W28

- Figma Community AI kits are shifting from generic chat screens toward production-ready agent patterns: streaming states, reasoning steps, tool calls, plan/confirm flows, citations, and approval states.
- Figma Make and agent-oriented kits reinforce a design-system view of AI: structured components and variables rather than novelty prompts.
- Portfolio implication: use state rails, progress-like affordances, and calm token discipline as visual language; do not turn the portfolio into a chat interface.
