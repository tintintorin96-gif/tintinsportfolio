## Emerging signals observed

- Identity behaving as morphing material (Lama Lama DNA metamorphosis)
- Archive and present work sharing one craft stage (Partizan living reel + classics)
- Technical texture as focus language inside two-color frames (Dragonfly ASCII hover)
- Process environments replacing passive brand pages (Lacoste Polo Factory)
- Showreels becoming spatial showcases (Noomo Showcase; Jesko Jets / Cipher prestige precision)

## Chosen creative direction

**Living Material Stage** — The hero becomes a living material stage where identity morphs through typographic hierarchy, and archive presence shares the same continuum as current craft without becoming a showreel or dashboard.

## Hero concept

Overscaled stacked type occupies a cinematic band as living material — weight and tracking shift on scroll as one morphing identity thread. A thin oxide material rule and a bounded technical texture reveal on focus mark craft presence; archive-like fragment labels stay subordinate to hierarchy.

## How the rest of the page inherits

- Supporting sections use muted mineral bands and bordered cards
- Oxide accent appears only on interactive affordances and navigation underline
- Work grid stays calm and scannable; no immersive takeover
- Navigation remains structurally stable
- Content and case study copy are unchanged

## Avoid

- Warm cream editorial palettes with serif magazine hierarchy
- Full-site gamification or factory metaphors that obscure scanning
- ASCII or shader overlays covering body text or links
- Unbounded WebGL showreels without readable entry points
- Chat-dashboard or agent-chrome mimicry
- Motion without an equivalent static hierarchy under reduced motion
