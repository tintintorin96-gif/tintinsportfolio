# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W37

**Range:** 2026-09-07 — 2026-09-13  
**Research window:** 2026-08-31 — 2026-09-06

## Direction

Earned Blueprint

## Concept

The hero is the first step of a blueprint walk — one thesis mark, two-color linework, and type that leads. Motion is earned because the field stays calm.

## Hero

Split-grid as a drafting sheet: overscaled stacked type beside a single hairline blueprint rule and thesis mark. Cool zinc paper (`#F5F6F3`) and deep blueprint ink (`#0F3D8C`). Scroll advances the walk; objects stay marks, not 3D products. Experimental only in the hero band.

## Emerging signals

- Agentic narrative as a single walk — Razorpay Sprint26 (Awwwards case Aug 31), United Carriers (Awwwards SOTD Sep 6)
- Two-color blueprint discipline — Sprint26 `#0039FF` / `#151515`; United Carriers `#0016CB`; MECHA non-fiction grid (CSSDA WOTD Sep 2)
- DOM-as-architecture, material-as-finish — Jesper Landberg (CSSDA WOTD Aug 31)
- Adaptive quality: perception over simulation — Vercel Prism / vgpu (Codrops Sep 3)
- High-contrast identity systems as supporting calm — Squarespace Foundations (Awwwards SOTD Sep 1), Paul Kalkbrenner (Awwwards SOTD Sep 2)

## Visual language

Cool zinc drafting paper, near-black ink, hard corners, hairline rules. Typographic hierarchy as primary texture. One deep blueprint accent for the thesis mark. Not cream editorial, not last week’s wine-ink chamber, not instrument-steel sapphire.

## Interaction and motion

Linear-reveal storytelling. Default cursor and color-shift hover as ink meeting paper. Cinematic scroll only as the walk in the hero. No page transition staging. Subtle intensity. Navigation stays a stable lintel. Instant states under prefers-reduced-motion.

## How the rest of the page inherits

- Supporting sections (60%): grid-rhythm, balanced spacing, the same zinc paper/ink — quieter drafting after the first step
- Work grid (30%): bordered teasers, color-shift only — infrastructure as index, not 3D unboxing
- Navigation (10%): minimal border lintel, no structural change
- Content and case studies (0%): not rewritten

## Avoid

- Full WebGL freight yards, walkable houses, and automotive showrooms
- Giant 3D product objects as the entire hero (Razorpay shoe)
- 100+ Rive state-machine triggers as a portfolio default
- Mediterranean and performer pastels (ERA Residence, Trevor Noah)
- Jewellery configurator worlds (Heirest)
- Real-time datamosh and playground physics heroes
- ASCII-print WebGL as the default canvas
- Warm cream editorial palettes and serif magazine layouts
- Wine-ink cinematic chambers from W36
- Snap-sections paired with expressive motion
