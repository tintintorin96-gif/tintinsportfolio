# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W39

**Range:** 2026-09-21 — 2026-09-27  
**Research window:** 2026-09-14 — 2026-09-20

## Direction

Outline Gravity

## Concept

The hero treats hierarchy as a selection system — one line holds gravity while adjacent type recedes to architectural outline, with motion as a second, interruptible layer.

## Hero

Overscaled stacked type on a cool kiln-ash field (`#E7ECE6`) with kiln ink (`#121614`) and one deep pine accent (`#0E3F32`). Hover or scroll concentrates weight on the primary line while supporting lines drop to outline stroke. Experimental only in the hero band.

## Emerging signals

- Choice as interface architecture — same work, different modes of looking — Kononenko Architectural Bureau Codrops (Sep 18); House of Yellow Codrops (Sep 16)
- Hover that gives the active piece gravity while neighbors recede to outline — KNKO hover/outline system
- Motion as a second design layer on a restrained visual system — KNKO; House of Yellow scroll-as-timeline
- Directed first-contact as process state, not a chat overlay — L.I.S.A. (Awwwards SOTD Sep 16)
- Two-color kiln / outline fields after last week's glacier horizon — Aspen Search (Sep 15), Pensatori Irrazionali (Sep 20)

## Visual language

Cool kiln-ash plaster, kiln ink, tight drawing radius, hairline borders. Typographic gravity as primary texture. One deep pine mark for the active line. Not cream editorial, not last week's glacier/horizon cyan, not zinc/blueprint split-grid, not orange chroma.

## Interaction and motion

Panel-sequence storytelling. Highlight cursor as a selection mark; depth-reveal hover as outline recession. Standard scroll — last week's cinematic survey stays off. Crossfade page transition at subtle intensity. Navigation stays a stable lintel. Instant last-frame states under prefers-reduced-motion.

## How the rest of the page inherits

- Supporting sections (60%): grid-rhythm bands, balanced spacing, the same kiln plaster/ink — quieter list-mode after the gravity well
- Work grid (30%): bordered teasers, depth-reveal only — index as outline, not a morphing gallery
- Navigation (10%): minimal-border lintel, no structural change
- Content and case studies (0%): not rewritten
