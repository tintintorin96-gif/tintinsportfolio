# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W23

**Range:** 2026-06-02 — 2026-06-08

## Direction
Minimal Bold Signal

## Concept
Stark typographic hierarchy and one confident accent reinterpret the hero as a signal, not decoration.

## Hero
Overscaled headline, tight tracking, accent rule beneath the greeting — experimental only in the hero band.

## Emerging signals
- High-contrast neutrals with single accent discipline
- Tight geometric radii and hard borders
- Typographic weight as primary visual texture
- Subtle motion limited to interactive affordances

## Visual language
Direct, high-contrast, minimal surface noise — clarity over atmosphere.

## Avoid
- Editorial serif display
- Warm cream editorial palettes
- Decorative gradients
- Short-lived gimmick effects
