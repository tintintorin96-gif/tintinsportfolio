## Direction

Use a warm technical editorial system:

- Background: parchment warmth.
- Foreground: deep ink.
- Accent: grounded copper that remains readable on light surfaces.
- Surface language: tactile borders, soft shadow depth, precise spacing.
- Motion language: subtle, directed, and optional.
- Signature behavior: one memorable hero motif reused with restraint.

The site should feel professional and current, not like a trend demo. Content remains the center of gravity.
