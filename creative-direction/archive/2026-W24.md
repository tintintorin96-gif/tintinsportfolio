# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W24

**Range:** 2026-06-09 — 2026-06-15

## Direction
Cinematic Signal Observatory

## Concept
A dark spatial hero frames the portfolio as an inspectable signal room, where bold type, copper coordinates, and restrained scroll depth guide attention without changing content.

## Hero
The hero behaves like a low-lit observatory: oversized sans type anchors the scene while a subtle coordinate grid, amber scan line, and depth-reveal hover states suggest live systems under human direction.

## Emerging signals
- Scroll as camera-state controller for chaptered spatial stories
- Reusable GSAP and Webflow motion systems replacing one-off animation stunts
- AI-native interfaces exposing traces, confidence, and override states
- Two-dimensional hierarchy balanced with restrained WebGL-inspired depth
- Luxury-tech restraint through dark fields and copper signal accents

## Visual language
Dark luxury-tech field, warm ivory type, copper signal accents, and faint coordinate structure create depth while preserving minimal bold hierarchy.

## Interaction and motion language
- Scroll behaves cinematically but remains subtle and reversible.
- Hover states feel like inspection: a controlled depth reveal or trace highlight.
- Navigation remains stable, inheriting only a restrained accent underline.
- Reduced motion uses static coordinate depth, instant states, and underline-only feedback.

## Supporting page inheritance
- Supporting sections inherit grid rhythm, elevated surfaces, and copper trace details.
- Work cards receive light depth treatment only; project access and teaser structure remain unchanged.
- Content, navigation structure, routing, and case study text are not changed.

## Avoid
- Full-site game world metaphors
- Generic particle atmospheres
- Open-ended AI chat motifs
- Serif editorial magazine styling
- Motion that hides text or disorients scrolling
