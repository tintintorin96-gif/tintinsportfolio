# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W36

**Range:** 2026-08-31 — 2026-09-06  
**Research window:** 2026-08-24 — 2026-08-30

## Direction

Chaptered Chamber

## Concept

The hero becomes the first chamber of a maison-index — chaptered typography and object-led reveals, not a walkable WebGL house.

## Hero

Cinematic band as a reading chamber: overscaled stacked type, a single chapter rule that extends on entry, cool stone paper (`#EEF1F4`) and wine-ink (`#5C2148`). Scroll advances the prologue; objects stay indexed, not immersive. Experimental only in the hero band.

- Chaptered editorial as spatial architecture — AI in Design Report 2026 (Awwwards SOTD Aug 26), /zeroz chapters (Aug 24), Aardvark Book Club (Aug 30)
- Object-led discovery as a readable index — MIU MIU A House that we shaped (Awwwards SOTD Aug 25); Codrops square-lens frame (Aug 25)
- Maison / atelier luxury through identity, space, and digital restraint — Maison AUGE (CSSDA WOTD Aug 30)
- Data, analysis, and motion as one language — AI in Design editorial charts; Sharplink (Awwwards SOTD Aug 27)
- High-contrast monochrome supporting calm — HOBRO DIGITAL (Awwwards SOTD Aug 29)

## Visual language

Cool chamber paper, chamber ink, tight architectural radii. Typographic rooms. One wine-ink accent for chapter emphasis. Not cream editorial, not last week’s instrument steel + sapphire.

## Interaction and motion

Chapter-scroll storytelling. Highlight cursor and depth-reveal hover as object-as-index. Crossfade between chambers. Subtle intensity. Navigation stays a stable lintel. Instant states under prefers-reduced-motion.

## How the rest of the page inherits

- Supporting sections (60%): muted bands, airy rhythm, the same cool paper/ink — quieter chambers after the prologue
- Work grid (30%): bordered teasers, depth-reveal only — catalog as index, not 3D unboxing
- Navigation (10%): minimal border lintel, no structural change
- Content and case studies (0%): not rewritten

## Avoid

- Full WebGL walkable houses and immersive 3DCG chapter worlds
- 90s pastel retro capsules (Decathlon Yestalgia)
- Jiggly CTA and ear-popping hover gimmicks
- Comic-book full-world brand universes (Santioni Notturno)
- Warm cream editorial palettes and serif magazine layouts
- Snap-sections paired with expressive motion
- Mouse-following RGB lens as the default cursor
- Portfolio boot sequences and spinning-disc intros
