## Direction

Survey Horizon

## Concept

The hero is a surveyed horizon — typographic bearing on a glacier field, one course-line accent — so the rest of the page can sit as quieter waypoints.

## Hero

Asymmetric cinematic composition: overscaled type offset like a heading indicator above a single horizon rule. Cool glacier vapor (`#E4EEF1`) and deep horizon cyan (`#0A5366`). Scroll advances the survey; objects stay bearings, not 3D vessels or cockpits. Experimental only in the hero band.

## Visual language

Cool glacier vapor paper, polar ink, tight instrument radius, one horizon rule. Typographic bearing as primary texture. One deep horizon cyan for the course-line. Not cream editorial, not last week's zinc/blueprint split-grid, not wine-ink chamber, not instrument-steel sapphire.

## Interaction and motion

Chapter-scroll storytelling. Highlight cursor as a bearing mark; depth-reveal hover as ice layers. Cinematic scroll only as the survey in the hero. Crossfade page transition at subtle intensity. Navigation stays a stable lintel. Instant last-frame states under prefers-reduced-motion.

## How the rest of the page inherits

- Supporting sections (60%): muted bands, airy spacing, the same glacier paper/ink — quieter waypoints after the horizon
- Work grid (30%): elevated teasers, depth-reveal only — itinerary as index, not 3D unboxing
- Navigation (10%): stable-flat lintel, no structural change
- Content and case studies (0%): not rewritten

- Zinc drafting paper, split-grid, and blueprint navy from last week
- Warm cream editorial palettes and serif magazine layouts
- Wine-ink cinematic chambers and instrument-steel sapphire
- Snap-sections paired with expressive motion
