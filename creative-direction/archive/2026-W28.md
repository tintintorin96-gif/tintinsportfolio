# Weekly creative direction - 2026-W28

**Date range:** 2026-07-06 - 2026-07-12  
**Signal window:** 2026-06-29 - 2026-07-05  
**Creative direction:** Protocol-Lit Cinematics

## One-sentence concept

The hero becomes a restrained cinematic control surface where typography, state rails, and tactile hover cues make the portfolio feel like a transparent AI-native system without changing content.

## Emerging signals observed

- Awwwards SOTD examples from Jul 1-3 (Wembi, Units, Bucks Sauce) emphasized controlled navigation/menu choreography, scrollytelling pages, hover details, product sliders, and tactile object moments.
- CSS Design Awards surfaced a Week 28 cluster around AI/creative-tech showcases, including Icare - A WorldLabs Showcase and AI-adjacent nominees.
- AG-UI, AWS AgentCore, and CopilotKit showed AI-native interfaces moving toward visible typed event streams, tool-call states, shared canvases, and human approval pauses.
- Codrops' Podium case study reinforced restraint: scroll as narrative pacing, soft directional transitions, strong typography, and WebGL used as material texture rather than spectacle.
- Webflow and Framer motion tooling continued making GSAP-grade scroll, SplitText, and sticky sequence patterns more accessible, raising baseline expectations for polished portfolio motion.

## Chosen direction

Protocol-Lit Cinematics translates those signals into a luxury-tech portfolio system. The direction borrows the trust language of AI agent interfaces - visible state, artifacts, and controlled progression - while preserving the site's content and information architecture.

The result should feel precise, cinematic, and system-aware: dark high-contrast surfaces, mint signal accents, glass-safe panels, and motion that clarifies sequence rather than demanding attention.

## Hero concept

A dark full-bleed hero stages oversized type beside luminous status rails and artifact-like panels. Scroll advances the rails in calm beats while pointer hover exposes contained depth.

## Inheritance model

- **Hero (100% freedom):** Full luxury-tech canvas with luminous rails, panelized artifacts, subdued shader haze, and cinematic chapter-scroll pacing.
- **Supporting sections (60% freedom):** Inherit the grid rhythm, dark surfaces, mint affordance accents, and restrained reveal timing.
- **Work grid (30% freedom):** Treat cards as quiet artifacts with glass-safe depth and readable borders.
- **Navigation (10% freedom):** Keep structure stable; add only a subtle accent underline and high-contrast focus states.
- **Content and case studies (0% freedom):** No copy, routing, data, or teaser changes.

## Token and interaction notes

- Hero variant: `luxury-tech`
- Hero layout: `split-grid`
- Motion intensity: `subtle`
- Scroll behavior: `cinematic`
- Cursor treatment: `highlight`
- Hover behavior: `depth-reveal`
- Reduced motion: static rails and panels, no shader movement or scroll-scrubbed animation

## What to avoid

- Literal chat UI or agent mascots.
- Full-screen WebGL scenes that obscure reading.
- Cursor followers that compete with navigation.
- Low-contrast neon-on-dark treatments.
- Autoplay loops, flashing status effects, or scroll-jacked panels.

## Accessibility guardrails

- Foreground/background tokens use high contrast.
- Accent is used for UI signals and remains above the 3:1 accent threshold on the dark background.
- Body text remains minimal and highly legible.
- Motion stays subtle and must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
