# Weekly Creative Direction — 2026-W28

**Range:** 2026-07-06 — 2026-07-12

## Direction

Intent-Led Signal Atelier

## Concept

The hero becomes a luxury-tech signal atelier where intent, state, and narrative hierarchy feel actively curated while the rest of the portfolio remains calm and stable.

## Emerging signals observed

- **Pageless intent-aware journeys:** Brunello Cucinelli AI E-com and Callimacus case material point to luxury interfaces that assemble experiences around live intent rather than fixed page funnels.
- **Structured agent-state surfaces:** AG-UI/A2UI references and Figma Community agent kits show AI interfaces moving toward visible tool calls, state deltas, approvals, and streaming progress.
- **Cinematic luxury restraint:** Depo Luxe and Brunello Cucinelli show technology as hospitality: quiet, curated, and human rather than dashboard-heavy.
- **Archival scene storytelling:** 21 Hrs On The Moon and 100 Lost Species use atmosphere, evidence, and sequence to create discovery.
- **Interaction as decision clarity:** Dolce & Gabbana Beauty Gift Finder, RISK, and configurator-led product launches use interaction to clarify fit, risk, or consequence.

## Hero concept

A moonlit graphite hero with asymmetric stacked type and a luminous signal rail. The canvas suggests an interface that listens, calibrates, and reveals state without becoming a chat surface or changing portfolio content.

## Visual language

Luxury-tech restraint:

- graphite background
- warm high-contrast foreground
- one luminous blue accent
- calibrated borders and soft shadowed surfaces
- structured signal rhythm instead of decorative gradients

## Interaction and motion language

- Cinematic scroll pacing, but no snap-heavy sections
- Depth-reveal hover for important surfaces
- Subtle state confirmation in the hero only
- Stable navigation with an accent underline
- Reduced motion resolves instantly to clear hierarchy and static signal state

## Inheritance model

- **Hero (100% freedom):** carries the full signal atelier concept through asymmetric composition, luminous accent, and luxury-tech mood.
- **Supporting sections (60% freedom):** inherit grid rhythm, elevated surfaces, and calmer state-like structure.
- **Work grid (30% freedom):** gets depth and border treatment only; no content rewrite.
- **Navigation (10% freedom):** remains structurally stable with subtle accent underline.
- **Content and case studies (0% freedom):** unchanged.

## Supporting page treatment

Supporting sections should feel like quiet chapters after the hero prologue: grid-rhythm backgrounds, elevated cards, readable type, and restrained luminous affordances.

## Avoid

- Full-screen AI chat metaphors for the portfolio
- Opaque personalization or content changes
- Heavy WebGL worlds that hide readable content
- Neon cyber gradients without narrative purpose
- Cursor gimmicks or snap-heavy motion
